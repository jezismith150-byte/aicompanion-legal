# Delete your AI Companion account

AI Companion is operated by Ali Raza, Pakistan.

## Delete it in the app

1. Open AI Companion.
2. Go to the **Account** tab.
3. Tap **Delete my account** and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

## Can't open the app?

Email **jezismith150@gmail.com** from the email address saved on your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

## What is deleted

- Your account (account ID, email address and password)
- All your chats
- Your coin balance and unlocked characters
- Your purchase records in our database

Unused coins are lost when the account is deleted and cannot be refunded.

## What may be kept

- Reports you made about AI replies, in anonymised form (not linked to you), for safety and legal purposes.
- Google Play and RevenueCat keep their own payment records as required by law and their policies.

If you only want to delete your chats, use **Account → Delete all chat history** instead.
