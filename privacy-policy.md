# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

AI Companion ("the app", "we") is operated by Ali Raza, Pakistan. Contact: jezismith150@gmail.com.

## What we collect

- **Account data.** A random account ID created when you first open the app. If you choose to save your account, your email address and a password (stored only as a secure hash).
- **Chats.** The messages you send and the AI replies, stored so your conversations continue across sessions and devices.
- **Coins and purchases.** Your coin balance, characters you unlocked, and records of coin purchases (product, amount, transaction ID). Payments are handled by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details.
- **Reports.** If you report an AI reply, we store the reported message and the character it came from.
- **Age confirmation.** Whether you confirmed that you are 18 or older (required to use the app).

We do not use advertising trackers, we do not show ads, and we do not sell your data.

## How we use it

To run the app (show your chats, keep your coins, unlock characters), to generate AI replies, to credit coins you buy, to prevent abuse and fraud, to review safety reports, and to provide support.

## Who processes your data

We use these service providers only to run the app. They process data on our behalf and not for their own advertising.

- **Supabase** hosts our database and authentication ([supabase.com/privacy](https://supabase.com/privacy)).
- **Google (Gemini API)** receives the text of your recent messages and the character's instructions so it can generate replies ([policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)). Do not share sensitive personal information in chats.
- **Google Play** processes your payments ([policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)).
- **RevenueCat** receives your account ID and purchase records so we can verify purchases and credit coins ([revenuecat.com/privacy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)).

## AI characters

Every character is an AI, not a real person. Replies are generated automatically and may be inaccurate or inappropriate. Characters are not a substitute for professional medical, legal, financial or mental-health advice.

## Your choices

- **Delete chats:** Account tab, Delete all chat history.
- **Delete your account:** Account tab, Delete my account. This permanently removes your account, coins, unlocked characters, purchase records and chats from our database. If you cannot open the app, email jezismith150@gmail.com from the email saved on your account and we will delete it within 30 days.
- To request a copy of your data or ask a privacy question, email jezismith150@gmail.com.

## Children

The app is only for people aged 18 and over. You confirm your age when you first open the app. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; if you believe a minor is using the app, email jezismith150@gmail.com and we will delete the account.

## Retention and security

We keep your data until you delete it or your account. Data is encrypted in transit. Reports may be kept in anonymised form after account deletion for safety and legal purposes. Google Play and RevenueCat may keep their own records of payments as required by law and their policies.

## Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
